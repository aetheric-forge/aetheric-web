# Tracking

## Definition

**Tracking** is the capability through which Parallel You maintains useful continuity concerning states, Activities, Events, Outcomes, and changes over Time.

Tracking relates captured information and Observations to the representations they may describe, while preserving temporal scope, provenance, uncertainty, and Context.

Tracking may help the Person understand what is current, what changed, what remains unresolved, and what may require attention.

Tracking is not continuous surveillance, perfect observation, or proof of the Person's experience.

## Purpose

Tracking allows Parallel You to:

- preserve relevant state across Time;
- record change without requiring the Person to remember every detail;
- distinguish expected, reported, observed, inferred, and unknown conditions;
- maintain continuity across Plans, Activities, Intentions, and Commitments;
- recognize Events, transitions, and unresolved conditions;
- support timely Planning and Recommendation;
- provide evidence for Reflection;
- and detect when retained representations may no longer be current.

The purpose of Tracking is not to maximize measurement or behavioural visibility.

Its purpose is to preserve enough trustworthy continuity to support the Person with less cognitive and administrative burden.

## Scope

Tracking may concern:

- Intentions and Commitments;
- Plans and Recommendations;
- Activities, Tasks, Events, and Outcomes;
- Time, availability, and recurrence;
- Energy and Attention States;
- conditions, constraints, dependencies, and resources;
- Context, location, or environment where appropriate;
- delegated actions and external processes;
- unresolved questions, risks, and review points;
- and the currentness or quality of system representations.

Not everything represented by Parallel You should be Tracked.

Tracking should occur only where continuity or change provides sufficient value.

## Tracking and Capture

Capture receives and preserves information.

Tracking associates information with a subject across Time and maintains an operational understanding of change or continuity.

A captured item may contribute to Tracking without conclusively determining tracked state.

Tracking may identify that additional Capture or clarification is useful.

Parallel You shall not treat every captured Expression or Observation as a state transition.

## Tracking and Observation

An Observation provides evidence concerning a condition, Event, Activity, state, or Outcome.

Tracking organizes relevant Observations across Time.

An Observation may be:

- explicitly reported;
- directly recorded;
- imported;
- instrumented;
- derived;
- inferred;
- or unknown in origin.

The system shall preserve source, temporal scope, confidence, and limitations where they affect interpretation.

One Observation shall not automatically define a persistent state.

## Tracking and Reflection

Tracking preserves and organizes evidence.

Reflection examines evidence to develop understanding.

Tracking may identify differences, recurrence, or apparent Patterns without determining their meaning.

Reflection may reinterpret tracked information or change what the Person considers worth Tracking.

Parallel You shall not present descriptive summaries, trends, or deviations as explanations merely because tracked data makes them visible.

## Tracking and Planning

Planning may use tracked state to understand current conditions, unresolved work, available capacity, dependencies, or relevant change.

Tracking may use a Plan to identify expected Activities, Events, Outcomes, review points, and conditions.

A Plan describes a proposed course of action. Tracking describes available evidence concerning state and change.

Difference from a Plan shall not automatically be classified as Tracking error, execution failure, or a need for correction.

Tracking shall not silently modify a Plan merely to make observed action appear compliant.

## Tracking and Recommendation

Tracking may provide evidence that a Recommendation is timely, stale, unnecessary, or potentially useful.

A Recommendation may suggest that the Person Track something where doing so has clear value.

Tracked difference, threshold crossing, or missing information shall not automatically authorize a Recommendation.

Parallel You should consider relevance, burden, uncertainty, and the Person's current Context before turning tracked information into interruption or advice.

## Tracking and Monitoring

**Monitoring** is repeated or ongoing observation of a tracked subject for relevant change.

Tracking may occur without continuous Monitoring.

Monitoring may be:

- periodic;
- event-driven;
- conditional;
- manually initiated;
- externally supplied;
- or active only during a defined interval.

Continuous Monitoring shall not be presumed necessary merely because it is technically possible.

The frequency and intrusiveness of Monitoring should remain proportional to purpose, sensitivity, rate of change, and consequence.

## Tracking and Measurement

Measurement assigns a value, category, range, count, or other operational representation to an observed property.

Tracking may use measurement, but not every tracked subject is meaningfully measurable.

Qualitative state, narrative description, uncertainty, and absence of information may all be legitimate tracked representations.

Numerical representation shall not imply objective units, precision, linearity, or comparability where those properties are unsupported.

Parallel You shall not force the Person's experience into a scale merely to make it easier to graph or optimize.

## Subject

A **Tracked Subject** is the representation, condition, process, relationship, or question for which continuity over Time is being maintained.

A Tracked Subject may be:

- a domain object;
- a property or state;
- an Activity Occurrence;
- an expected Event or Outcome;
- a dependency or condition;
- a recurring obligation;
- an external process;
- or an unresolved matter.

The Subject and its scope should be sufficiently clear for observations to be interpreted without demanding unnecessary detail.

Similar names shall not, by themselves, establish that Observations concern the same Subject.

## State

A **Tracked State** is the system's provisional understanding of a Subject within a temporal and contextual scope.

A Tracked State may include:

- a value, range, category, or description;
- applicable Time or Interval;
- Context;
- provenance;
- confidence;
- known uncertainty;
- relevant conditions;
- and the Observation or reasoning that supports it.

A Tracked State is a representation, not an intrinsic property of the Person or the world.

Where no current state is supported, the state should remain unknown rather than be silently carried forward.

## State Transition

A **State Transition** represents that the system has evidence of relevant change from one state to another.

A State Transition may reflect:

- change in the represented subject;
- correction of an earlier record;
- refinement of uncertainty;
- changed classification;
- or changed interpretation.

These possibilities shall remain distinguishable where they affect meaning.

The receipt of new information does not necessarily mean the represented subject changed at that moment.

## Current State

**Current State** is the best supported representation applicable to the present operational Context.

Current State may be:

- directly reported;
- recently observed;
- derived from a continuing condition;
- inferred from prior state and elapsed Time;
- forecast;
- stale;
- disputed;
- or unknown.

The latest recorded value is not necessarily the Current State.

Parallel You shall consider represented Time, validity, Context, and intervening evidence rather than selecting records solely by creation timestamp.

## Historical State

A **Historical State** represents the system's understanding of a Subject at an earlier Time.

Historical State may remain incomplete or uncertain even after the Time has passed.

Correction of historical information may revise the record while preserving that a previous representation existed and may have influenced action.

Parallel You shall not silently rewrite history merely to maintain consistency with Current State.

Changes in present understanding should remain distinguishable from changes that occurred in the represented world.

## Expected State

An **Expected State** represents what a Plan, Commitment, recurrence, external process, or other source anticipates may apply at a future Time or condition.

Expected State may guide Monitoring or review.

It is not an Observation or Current State.

Failure of an Expected State to occur may indicate changed conditions, inaccurate assumptions, uncertainty, or an expected but unobserved Event.

Parallel You shall not convert expectation into history merely because its anticipated Time has passed.

## Derived State

A **Derived State** is computed or inferred from other information.

Examples may include:

- a Commitment appearing at risk because a Deadline approaches;
- a Plan appearing blocked because a dependency remains unresolved;
- an Activity appearing interrupted because a new incompatible Event occurred;
- or an Energy estimate derived from recent reports and Activities.

Derived State shall preserve its supporting evidence, method, assumptions, confidence, and applicable scope where those properties affect use.

A Derived State shall not be represented as directly observed.

## Unknown State

An **Unknown State** represents insufficient evidence to support a more specific current representation.

Unknown is not equivalent to absent, false, inactive, incomplete, or unchanged.

Unknown State may arise because:

- the Subject was not observed;
- evidence conflicts;
- applicable Time or Context is unclear;
- the last known state expired;
- access is unavailable;
- or Tracking was intentionally limited.

Parallel You should preserve Unknown State when resolving it would impose more burden or intrusion than its value justifies.

## Temporal Scope

Every Tracked State applies within some temporal scope, even when that scope is uncertain.

Temporal scope may concern:

- an Instant;
- an Interval;
- a recurrence Occurrence;
- a condition;
- the period until invalidated;
- or an unknown duration.

Capture Time, observation Time, occurrence Time, effective Time, and processing Time may differ.

The system shall not silently use record creation Time as the effective Time of a state.

## Contextual Scope

A Tracked State may apply only within a Context.

For example, Energy, Attention, availability, Activity demand, authority, or Commitment status may differ across:

- environments;
- roles;
- relationships;
- Projects;
- health conditions;
- and types of Activity.

Parallel You shall not generalize a state beyond its supported Context merely to simplify the model.

Contexts may overlap, and different scoped states may legitimately coexist.

## Continuity

Tracking may infer that a state continues between Observations.

Continuity may be supported by:

- the nature of the Subject;
- an explicit validity Interval;
- absence of an expected terminating Event;
- recurrence rules;
- or a defined operational assumption.

Continuity is not universally safe to assume.

The system should represent how long a state may reasonably be carried forward and when it becomes stale or unknown.

Silence shall not automatically establish continuity.

## Validity and Expiration

A Tracked State may possess a validity condition or expiration.

Validity may end because:

- a specified Time passes;
- a new Observation supersedes it;
- its Context changes;
- a terminating Event occurs;
- the supporting source becomes invalid;
- or confidence decays beyond useful support.

Expiration does not establish that the opposite state is true.

An expired state ordinarily becomes historical or unknown unless other evidence supports a current representation.

## Staleness

**Staleness** describes reduced confidence that retained information still represents the Subject in the applicable present Context.

Staleness depends upon:

- elapsed Time;
- expected rate of change;
- volatility;
- Context;
- source reliability;
- consequence of error;
- and intervening evidence.

Information is not stale solely because it is old, nor current solely because it is recent.

Parallel You should make material Staleness visible before relying upon a state for consequential Planning or Recommendation.

## Frequency

Tracking Frequency describes how often information is sought, received, evaluated, or summarized.

Frequency may be:

- event-driven;
- fixed;
- adaptive;
- conditional;
- Person-initiated;
- or irregular.

Higher Frequency may improve temporal resolution while increasing burden, interruption, privacy exposure, noise, and storage.

Parallel You should use the lowest Frequency that provides sufficient value for the intended purpose.

## Granularity

Tracking Granularity describes the level of detail at which state or change is represented.

Granularity may concern:

- temporal resolution;
- categories or dimensions;
- individual Activity Occurrences;
- sub-states;
- Context;
- or provenance.

Greater granularity is not inherently more accurate or useful.

Parallel You should avoid collecting fine-grained information when a coarser representation adequately supports the Person.

## Baseline

A **Baseline** is a reference representation used for comparison.

A Baseline may be:

- an earlier state;
- a typical range;
- a Person-defined reference;
- a Plan assumption;
- an external standard;
- or a provisional estimate.

Baselines are contextual and revisable.

Parallel You shall preserve the source and meaning of a Baseline rather than presenting every difference as improvement or decline.

The Person's historical behaviour shall not automatically become a normative standard for future behaviour.

## Threshold

A **Threshold** is a condition at which tracked information may warrant review, notification, or another authorized response.

A Threshold may be:

- explicit;
- inferred;
- provisional;
- contextual;
- hard;
- or advisory.

Crossing a Threshold is evidence that its defined condition applies. It does not determine the meaning, urgency, cause, or appropriate response.

Thresholds should include sufficient hysteresis, timing, confidence, or review conditions to avoid unnecessary oscillation and noise where appropriate.

## Change Detection

**Change Detection** determines whether available evidence supports that a relevant state differs from an earlier or expected state.

Detected change may reflect:

- genuine change;
- measurement variation;
- different Context;
- changed Source;
- correction;
- missing data;
- or altered interpretation.

Parallel You should represent material uncertainty concerning which explanation applies.

Not every detectable difference is meaningful enough to preserve or surface.

## Event Detection

Tracking may identify that available evidence supports the occurrence of an Event.

Event Detection may depend upon:

- explicit reporting;
- external notification;
- a state transition;
- a threshold;
- a combination of Observations;
- or inference.

A detected Event shall preserve its provenance, estimated Time, confidence, and resolution to a represented subject.

The system shall not confuse detected system activity with an Event involving the Person unless the evidence supports that relationship.

## Recurrence

Tracking may maintain continuity for recurring Activities, Commitments, Events, or conditions.

A recurring definition is distinct from each expected, reported, or observed Occurrence.

Tracking should distinguish:

- a continuing recurrence definition;
- an expected Occurrence;
- an observed Occurrence;
- a skipped or cancelled Occurrence;
- an unobserved Occurrence;
- and a changed recurrence rule.

A missed or unknown Occurrence shall not automatically become accumulating debt.

## Progress

Tracking may represent evidence of Progress concerning a Plan, Activity, Commitment, Intention, or Outcome.

Progress may be:

- qualitative;
- partial;
- nonlinear;
- blocked;
- reversed;
- uncertain;
- or not meaningfully measurable.

Progress shall be defined relative to applicable purpose and criteria.

Parallel You shall not equate Progress universally with Task completion, elapsed Time, output volume, adherence, or increased Activity.

## Status

A **Status** is a concise operational representation of a Subject's current or recent state.

Status may help the Person identify what is active, waiting, blocked, uncertain, complete, or in need of review.

Status is a summary and may conceal relevant detail.

Parallel You should allow the evidence, scope, Time, and uncertainty behind a consequential Status to be inspected.

A Status label shall not silently acquire moral meaning.

## History

Tracking may preserve a History of Observations, states, transitions, Events, and corrections.

History supports:

- continuity;
- auditability;
- Reflection;
- estimate revision;
- and understanding how current representations developed.

History is necessarily incomplete.

Parallel You should avoid retaining detail indefinitely merely because it may someday be analyzable.

The Person shall not be defined or permanently constrained by tracked History.

## Summarization

Tracking may summarize information across Time, Subjects, or Contexts.

Summaries may include:

- current state;
- notable changes;
- unresolved items;
- recurrence Occurrences;
- ranges and distributions;
- or selected historical context.

Summarization involves selection and compression.

Parallel You shall not present a summary as complete where omitted detail, uncertainty, or Context could materially change its meaning.

## Notification

Tracking may identify information that could justify notifying the Person or another authorized Party.

A Notification should consider:

- relevance;
- urgency;
- consequence;
- confidence;
- timing;
- interruption cost;
- existing awareness;
- and delegated authority.

Detection does not require interruption.

Parallel You should avoid notifying the Person about every deviation, missing Observation, or Threshold crossing merely because it can.

## Tracking Burden

**Tracking Burden** is the Time, Energy, Attention, interruption, emotional effort, privacy exposure, and administrative work required to maintain tracked information.

Burden may arise through:

- repeated check-ins;
- manual logging;
- correction of inference;
- excessive Notifications;
- fine-grained measurement;
- duplicated records;
- or pressure to maintain a complete history.

Parallel You should account for both immediate and cumulative Tracking Burden.

The system shall not require more effort to Track an Activity or state than the resulting support is reasonably expected to return.

## Observability

**Observability** describes the degree to which relevant state or change can be known from available evidence.

Some Subjects are directly observable. Others are private, subjective, contextual, intermittent, or inferable only with substantial uncertainty.

Technical access does not guarantee meaningful Observability.

Parallel You shall not interpret low Observability as noncompliance, concealment, inactivity, or absence.

The system should prefer honest Unknown State to unsupported apparent visibility.

## Gaps

A **Tracking Gap** is an Interval or Context for which relevant evidence is absent or insufficient.

A Gap may result from:

- intentional non-Tracking;
- unavailable sources;
- technical failure;
- insufficient authority;
- reduced Person capacity;
- or information not being worth collecting.

A Gap shall not automatically be filled through interpolation or assumption.

Where continuity is inferred across a Gap, that inference shall remain visible and proportional to supporting evidence.

## Error and Correction

Tracking may contain errors involving:

- Subject resolution;
- state value;
- Time;
- Context;
- source;
- transitions;
- duplication;
- recurrence;
- or interpretation.

Correction should distinguish an error in the record from a change in the represented Subject.

Relevant prior representations and downstream effects should remain discoverable where needed for understanding or revision.

Correction shall not rewrite original Source material unless that Source is itself being corrected through an authorized process.

## Failure and Recovery

Tracking may fail because of unavailable sources, interrupted Monitoring, insufficient authority, incompatible data, unresolved identity, or technical error.

Parallel You should:

- preserve the last supported state with its Time and limitations;
- identify material Gaps;
- avoid false continuity;
- recover without duplicating or silently rewriting History;
- and communicate failure where it affects decisions or expected support.

Tracking failure shall not be attributed to the Person merely because they did not supply missing information.

## Consent and Authority

Tracking shall operate within bounded authority.

Authority may vary according to:

- Subject;
- Source;
- Frequency;
- granularity;
- Context;
- sensitivity;
- retention;
- audience;
- and permitted consequences.

Authority to access information does not necessarily authorize continuous Monitoring, durable History, cross-context combination, inference, Notification, or external disclosure.

The Person shall be able to inspect and restrict Tracking where appropriate.

## Privacy and Sensitivity

Tracking may reveal sensitive information through individual records, temporal detail, recurrence, location, relationships, or derived Patterns.

The combination of ordinary data may produce an unusually sensitive account.

Parallel You should minimize collection, frequency, granularity, retention, and combination according to actual purpose.

The system shall not treat data as harmless merely because each individual Observation appears mundane.

Sensitive tracked information shall not be exposed beyond its authorized Context or audience.

## Retention

Tracked information may be retained:

- until superseded;
- for a defined Interval;
- while a Plan or Commitment remains relevant;
- in summarized form;
- as selected History;
- or not beyond immediate evaluation.

Retention should reflect purpose, sensitivity, expected future value, correction needs, and the Person's authority.

Parallel You should distinguish the need for Current State from the need for detailed historical records.

Tracking does not justify indefinite retention merely because future analysis is conceivable.

## Lifecycle

Tracking of a Subject may be:

- proposed;
- authorized;
- active;
- intermittent;
- paused;
- degraded;
- awaiting information;
- restricted;
- completed;
- expired;
- revoked;
- or discontinued.

A Tracked State may separately be current, historical, expected, derived, disputed, stale, expired, corrected, or unknown.

Not every Subject requires continuous or permanent Tracking.

Lifecycle state shall preserve the distinction between the state of Tracking and the state of the Subject.

## Relationships

Tracking may:

- receive information through Capture and Observation;
- maintain state for Intentions, Commitments, Plans, Activities, Events, Outcomes, conditions, and resources;
- compare current, historical, expected, and derived states;
- detect change, Events, Gaps, Staleness, and Threshold conditions;
- maintain recurrence and Progress representations;
- support Planning, Recommendation, and Reflection;
- produce Status, summaries, and possible Notifications;
- and preserve History without defining the Person by it.

These relationships support continuity and timely assistance. They do not make Tracking a complete account of the Person or their life.

## Principles

- Tracking preserves useful continuity concerning state and change.
- Tracking is distinct from Capture, Monitoring, Measurement, Reflection, and surveillance.
- Current, historical, expected, derived, stale, and unknown states remain distinguishable.
- Temporal scope, Context, provenance, and uncertainty form part of every meaningful tracked state.
- The latest record is not necessarily the Current State.
- Unknown is a legitimate state, not a defect to be concealed.
- Greater Frequency, granularity, and retention are not inherently more useful or accurate.
- Tracking should use the least intrusive representation sufficient for its purpose.
- Detection does not require Notification, Recommendation, or intervention.
- History may inform assistance but shall not determine identity or destiny.
- Tracking exists to reduce cognitive burden and preserve agency, not enforce compliance.

## Invariants

Every implementation of Parallel You shall preserve the following invariants:

- A Tracked State shall not be treated as equivalent to the Person, the represented Subject, or an objective and complete account of reality.
- Tracking shall not be treated as equivalent to Capture, Monitoring, Measurement, Reflection, surveillance, or control.
- A captured item or single Observation shall not automatically establish a persistent state or State Transition.
- Reported, observed, imported, inferred, derived, assumed, and unknown states shall remain distinguishable where the difference affects use.
- The latest created or received record shall not automatically be represented as the Current State.
- Record creation Time shall not automatically be represented as observation, occurrence, or effective Time.
- Unknown State shall not be treated as absent, false, inactive, incomplete, unchanged, or noncompliant.
- Silence or lack of Observation shall not automatically establish continuity.
- Expiration of a state shall not establish that its opposite is true.
- Difference from a Baseline, Plan, Pattern, expectation, or earlier state shall not automatically be represented as improvement, decline, error, or failure.
- A Threshold crossing shall not, by itself, determine cause, urgency, meaning, Notification, Recommendation, or action.
- Numerical measurement shall not imply unsupported units, precision, linearity, comparability, or objectivity.
- A recurring definition shall remain distinct from expected, observed, skipped, cancelled, and unknown Occurrences.
- A missed or unobserved recurring Occurrence shall not automatically become accumulating debt.
- Progress shall not be universally equated with completion, adherence, output volume, elapsed Time, or increased Activity.
- A Tracking Gap shall not be silently filled through interpolation or assumption.
- Tracking History shall not be treated as an immutable property or destiny of the Person.
- Technical access to a Source shall not imply authority for continuous Monitoring, fine-grained Tracking, durable History, cross-context combination, inference, Notification, or disclosure.
- Detection of change, Staleness, risk, or missing information shall not automatically authorize interruption or action.
- Tracking shall not impose greater Time, Energy, Attention, emotional, privacy, or administrative burden than its expected value justifies.
- The Person shall retain appropriate authority to inspect, correct, restrict, pause, or discontinue Tracking.

# Person

## Definition

A **Person** is the human being whose life, intentions, capacity, and agency Parallel You exists to support.

The Person is the principal authority within the system. All plans, observations, recommendations, records, and automated actions derive their purpose and legitimacy from service to the Person.

A Person exists independently of any account, profile, device, dataset, plan, or representation maintained by Parallel You.

## Purpose

The Person concept establishes:

- whom the system serves;
- whose capacity is being represented;
- whose intentions give plans their meaning;
- whose authority governs recommendations and automation;
- and whose life must not be reduced to the system’s operational model.

## Person and User

A **User** is an operational identity interacting with the software.

A User may represent the Person directly, act on the Person’s behalf, administer the system, or operate through an authorized service or agent.

The Person and the User are therefore related but distinct.

A login session is not a Person.

An account is not a Person.

A User record is not a Person.

The system shall not infer that every action performed by an authenticated User directly expresses the Person’s intent.

## Person and Representation

Parallel You maintains representations of the Person and their circumstances.

These representations may include:

- identity information;
- preferences;
- responsibilities;
- commitments;
- availability;
- energy and attention states;
- activity history;
- planning behaviour;
- estimates;
- constraints;
- and inferred patterns.

No representation is the Person themselves.

A representation is necessarily partial and contextual. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate for a particular decision.

Parallel You shall preserve the distinction between:

- information explicitly provided by the Person;
- information observed by the system;
- information obtained from another source;
- information inferred from available evidence;
- and assumptions introduced for planning purposes.

## Agency

**Agency** is the Person’s capacity to form intentions, make decisions, revise commitments, and act within their circumstances.

Parallel You exists to increase practical agency by reducing administrative burden, preserving continuity, and making relevant choices easier to understand.

The system shall not treat predictability, compliance, activity, or adherence to a plan as substitutes for agency.

A Person may reject a recommendation without providing justification.

A Person may change an intention without being inconsistent.

A Person may abandon a commitment when circumstances, knowledge, or values change.

A Person may choose rest, exploration, play, or inactivity without those choices being interpreted as system failure.

## Capacity

A Person possesses finite and variable capacity.

Capacity may be affected by:

- available time;
- physical energy;
- cognitive energy;
- emotional energy;
- attention;
- health;
- environment;
- obligations;
- interruption;
- uncertainty;
- and recovery needs.

Capacity is contextual rather than absolute.

A Person may have sufficient capacity for one activity and insufficient capacity for another. Capacity observed at one time shall not be presumed to apply at another time or under different conditions.

Parallel You may estimate capacity, but such estimates remain provisional.

## Continuity

The Person persists across changes in:

- accounts;
- devices;
- schedules;
- preferences;
- responsibilities;
- health;
- habits;
- goals;
- and recorded history.

Parallel You shall preserve continuity where appropriate without treating prior behaviour as destiny.

Historical information may inform future assistance, but it shall not permanently constrain how the Person is represented or what the system believes them capable of doing.

## Context

A Person acts within multiple contexts.

Contexts may include:

- personal life;
- employment;
- education;
- family;
- community;
- health;
- creative work;
- institutional participation;
- and temporary circumstances.

A context may alter which intentions, constraints, resources, and responsibilities are relevant.

Contexts may overlap. They shall not be assumed to be mutually exclusive, structurally hierarchical, or equally important.

The system shall not infer that an intention belonging to one context is available to another without sufficient reason.

## Preference and Value

A **Preference** expresses how the Person presently favours one option, condition, or mode of interaction over another.

A **Value** expresses a more durable concern by which the Person may evaluate intentions, commitments, and choices.

Preferences and values may influence planning, but they do not mechanically determine action.

Both may change.

Parallel You shall avoid presenting inferred preferences or values as though the Person explicitly declared them.

## Delegation

The Person may delegate limited authority to Parallel You.

Delegation may permit the system to:

- organize information;
- propose plans;
- reschedule activities;
- create reminders;
- collect observations;
- perform reversible administrative actions;
- or interact with external systems.

Delegation shall be explicit enough to establish its purpose and scope.

Delegated authority shall not be presumed universal, permanent, or transferable.

The Person retains the authority to inspect, restrict, revoke, or override delegation.

## Relationships

A Person may:

- form one or more Intentions;
- undertake Activities;
- make or accept Commitments;
- possess available Capacity;
- act within one or more Contexts;
- create, accept, revise, or reject Plans;
- receive Recommendations;
- produce observable Events and Outcomes;
- delegate limited authority;
- and maintain a history without being defined by it.

These relationships describe the Person’s interaction with Parallel You. They do not constitute the Person.

## Principles

- The Person exists independently of the system.
- The Person is distinct from every account, User, session, profile, and dataset.
- The Person remains the principal authority over their intentions and choices.
- Representations of the Person are partial, contextual, and revisable.
- Human capacity is finite, variable, and activity-dependent.
- Historical behaviour may inform assistance but shall not determine identity or destiny.
- Agency takes precedence over adherence.
- Delegation is bounded and revocable.
- The system may support judgment but shall not replace the Person as the source of value.

## Invariants

Every implementation of Parallel You shall preserve the following invariants:

- A Person shall not be identified solely with an account, credential, User, session, or device.
- A model of the Person shall not be treated as equivalent to the Person.
- Inferred information shall remain distinguishable from declared or observed information.
- Uncertainty shall not be silently converted into fact.
- Authentication shall not imply unlimited authority to act on the Person’s behalf.
- Delegation shall remain bounded by purpose and scope.
- Rejection of a recommendation shall not diminish the Person’s authority.
- Deviation from a plan shall not, by itself, be interpreted as personal failure.
- Historical behaviour shall not be treated as an immutable property of the Person.
- The optimization goals of the software shall not supersede the Person’s agency.
